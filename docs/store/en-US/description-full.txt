Pickelhaube is a dialer for your FRITZ!Box. Pick the number comfortably on your smartphone – from your recent calls, your phone books or a speed dial – and your landline phone makes the call: the FRITZ!Box first rings the phone you chose, for example a DECT handset, and as soon as you pick it up, it dials the number. It works just like the dial help in the FRITZ!Box web interface, only much quicker.

CHOOSE A NUMBER
• Recent calls of the FRITZ!Box – dialled, received and missed, from all of its phones
• The phone books of the FRITZ!Box, with search, an A–Z bar and a filter by phone book
• Optionally your smartphone's own contacts next to them
• Speed dials 1–9 on your smartphone, valid with every box, plus the FRITZ!Box speed dials 00–99, which you can also set up and change from the app
• Suggestions while typing: matching numbers and names from your phone books and recent calls, also by the letters on the keys
• Share a text to Pickelhaube or paste it – the phone number in it is picked out and cleaned for dialling
• New missed calls of the FRITZ!Box are counted and marked
• Choose which phone rings: a single phone or all of them

DIALLING
• Hide your number for a single call
• With several own numbers on the FRITZ!Box: choose the one a call goes out from, and see in the recent calls which one each call was on
• Extensions, conference PINs and phone menus: the number is dialled, and the app tells you what to type once connected
• Internal calls between the phones of your FRITZ!Box – the kitchen from the study, for free
• A warning when an internet number of the FRITZ!Box is not registered with its provider

ON THE CALL
• Live call status: pick up, calling, connected with a running timer, call ended – based on the call monitor of the FRITZ!Box
• Cancel a call that is still being set up

CALL FORWARDING
• Switch the call forwardings set up on your FRITZ!Box on and off
• Also from a quick settings tile or a home screen shortcut, without opening the app

QUICK ACCESS
• Quick settings tiles: open the dialer, redial the last number, switch call forwarding
• Long press on the app icon: redial, call forwarding, choose a number, look for the FRITZ!Box
• Home screen widget with your own list of numbers – as many widgets as you like

SEVERAL FRITZ!BOXES
• Home, office, holiday flat: set up as many boxes as you like
• Pickelhaube finds out which box is in the network you are in and uses it automatically
• Works through a VPN as well

SECURE AND PRIVATE
• Talks to your FRITZ!Box only, using its official TR-064 interface – no cloud, no account, no advertising, no tracking
• Optional HTTPS with a pinned certificate, and support for confirming actions on the FRITZ!Box (two-factor)
• Passwords are stored encrypted in the Android Keystore
• Access to your contacts is optional, asked for only when you switch it on, and they never leave the phone
• Export and import all settings, passwords encrypted with a passphrase if you include them

LANGUAGES
English, German, Dutch, French, Italian, Spanish and Polish.

WHAT YOU NEED
• A FRITZ!Box with a telephone connected to it
• In the FRITZ!Box: "Allow access for applications" (Home Network › Network › Network Settings) and "Use dial help" (Telephony › Calls › Dial Help) switched on
• A FRITZ!Box user with the permission for telephony
• For the live call status: the call monitor of the box, switched on once by dialling #96*5* on a connected phone
